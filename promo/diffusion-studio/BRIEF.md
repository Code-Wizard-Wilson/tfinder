# TerFinder launch promo

## Goal

Create a 25-second, 1920x1080 launch film for X that makes the value obvious even with sound off: the user speaks naturally, TerFinder maps the request to a typed macOS action, and risky actions can be previewed without execution.

## Audience

Developers and power users who live in the terminal but do not want to memorize every command or trust a model to invent shell code.

## Story

1. Hook: the terminal should understand intent, not demand memorized syntax.
2. Diagnose: `tf почему мак тормозит` returns real process data.
3. Resolve: `tf что за PID 46272` identifies the exact process instead of treating the number as a port.
4. Breadth: Russian, English, and mixed phrasing resolve through one typed action system.
5. Trust: `tf plan выключи блютуз` shows intent, risk, planned action, and confirms no changes were made.
6. Lockup: TerFinder 0.6.0 — natural language in, typed actions out, local by default.

## Visual direction

- Near-black graphite background; off-white type; one terminal-green accent used only for successful recognition and safety.
- Geist / Geist Mono if available, with system fallbacks.
- One dominant terminal window, oversized kinetic headlines, restrained grid/radar texture, no generic floating cards.
- Hard cuts on action, short 400 ms entrances and 200 ms exits, continuous low-level motion during holds.
- All critical copy stays readable at mobile feed size.

## Audio

Minimal original electronic bed with low pulse, soft high-frequency ticks, and restrained impacts at story beats. No voiceover: the film must work muted and should not sound like synthetic narration.

## Delivery

- 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 MP4, AAC, ~25 seconds.
- Also keep the editable Diffusion Studio project and a contact sheet of verification frames.
